# Character art brief: the player and people of Aurelis

Same idea as the creature sheets: one image per sheet, **one character per row, four views** (FRONT, THREE-QUARTER, SIDE, BACK),
plain warm off-white background, no pedestals or scenery, generous space between views, small grey labels under each view,
the name on the left. Size 1774x887 (or 1536x1024). Attach a finished creature sheet as a style reference so people and
creatures look like they belong in the same world.

## Style rules (every character)
- Cute stylised 3D render, soft lighting, same look as the creature sheets.
- **Chibi proportions:** big head (about 1/3 of the height), small body, short limbs, big expressive eyes.
- **Standing, neutral pose**, arms relaxed at the sides, feet together, facing straight in FRONT and straight away in BACK.
- **Full body, nothing cropped, nothing held that hides the outline** (a staff or lamp is fine if it stays inside the silhouette).
- **Faces are different person to person:** eye shape, brows, nose, face shape, hairstyle and age, not just colours.
- Keep each character's colours (below) so they match how the game already shows them.

## How the game will use them
- **Overworld (walking around):** front, back and side views become the four walking directions (side is mirrored for left/right).
- **Battle intros:** the front view slides in when a trainer challenges you.
- **Dialogue portraits (optional later):** a head crop from the front view.

## What I need to wire them in (heads-up)
- People are smaller than creatures on screen. In the overworld they are about 20x25 game pixels, so simple, bold shapes read best.
- Walking animation: the game currently draws two stepping frames per direction. With still views I will use a gentle bob
  and squash for walking. If you want real stepping, add rows with "walk frame 1 / walk frame 2" for the player first.
- Unlisted trainer classes keep their current look until they get a sheet.
