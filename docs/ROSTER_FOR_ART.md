# Creature roster for reference sheets

One image per line below (one evolution line). Use these exact numbers, names and types.

## #1 Embrit -> #2 Emberon -> #3 Infernox
- **001 Embrit**: Fire. A playful fox kit. Its fluffy tail burns brighter whenever it is excited.
- **002 Emberon**: Fire. A proud fire fox. Its flame mane flares up when it means to fight.
- **003 Infernox**: Fire / Dark. Lava runs through its dark hide. It stalks the night with burning eyes.

## #4 Splashling -> #5 Torrentle -> #6 Tidalord
- **004 Splashling**: Water. It loves puddles and will follow rain clouds for miles.
- **005 Torrentle**: Water. Its fins flare into sprays of water as it darts through rivers.
- **006 Tidalord**: Water / Ground. A coiled river serpent. It stirs the mud of the riverbed into floods.

## #7 Sproutle -> #8 Thornling -> #9 Verdantus

- **008 Thornling**: Grass. Its leafy mane bristles when it guards its trainer.
- **009 Verdantus**: Grass / Rock. A great leaf-shelled beast. Ancient moss covers its stony back.

## #10 Fluffur -> #11 Fluffox

- **011 Fluffox**: Normal. A swift runner with a luxurious tail.
